# Foreword

This handout is aimed to be a reference for students trying to get a hang of various definitions in algebra. This is not very detailed nor does it contain any proofs, but contains everything which the author thinks is relevant for a general reference to frequently occurring definitions. The reader may read only the things they are currently familiar with or once were, so as to revise and recall or get a clearer idea of the concerned topic.
